# Hero boat visual references (racing mode)

**Purpose.** A curated study list for modelling the racing-mode hero boat: a 12 m LOA deep-V offshore monohull,
24° deadrise, ~80 kn, solo pilot, cockpit camera. In the fiction (flooded Sierra-Nevada archipelago, 2100) the
racers are **salvaged, rebuilt old offshore deep-V boats**: patched hulls, welded repairs, electric or biofuel
conversions, weathered decals, marine growth. These refs keep hull lines, cockpit, drives, materials and wear
consistent with each other. They are not a spec for copying any real or trademarked boat.

**Licence policy.**
- Refs are internal study material. They are never shipped and never fed to generators as recognisable
  trademarked boats. Thumbnails live only in `_local/`, which is gitignored.
- Manufacturer, editorial and forum images are "© owner, ref only". Wikimedia Commons licences are recorded
  per row (CC BY / BY-SA would need attribution if anything were ever derived from them, which is not the plan).
- The only material we may actually use in assets is CC0 texture libraries (ambientCG, Poly Haven), and those
  are flagged in (e).

**Verification.** On 2026-09-13 every non-Commons URL returned HTTP 200 to `curl` with a browser UA, and every Commons file was confirmed to exist through the Wikimedia API (which also gave the licence and author), with two exceptions.
Rows marked **†** returned 403 because the site blocks bots. Those links came straight from live search results
and should open fine in a browser. One dead link (p1offshore.com news) was dropped.
**Local** column: filename in `_local/` if a thumbnail was saved (≤ 800 px), otherwise blank.

**Rules envelope (APBA 2026 Offshore Rulebook, [PDF](https://www.apba.org/documents/219081930-07-26-2026-2026_APBA_OFFSHORE_RULEBOOK_7-18-2026.pdf))**
- Super Vee (twin-engine monohull): LOA 35'–46', beam ≤ 9'3", **keel-to-deck ≥ 48" (1.22 m) ahead of the canopy**, min weight 9,000 lb.
- Mod Vee (single-engine monohull): LOA 29'–32', beam ≤ 8'6", steps must be vented, min 4,750–5,000 lb.
- Canopy aperture ≥ 0.55 × 0.55 m per crew, corner radius 0.025 m, 20 mm fluorescent-orange band round the aperture.
- Head protection ≥ 0.2 m wide and ≥ 75 % of helmet height; ≥ 0.1 m canopy-to-crew clearance.
- Harness ≥ 5-point, belts ≥ 50 mm; shoulder straps 100–150 mm apart behind the neck.
- Windows: polycarbonate ≥ ½" (0.500") in Mod Vee; Super V recommended ¾" curved / 1¼" flat; UIM Class 1 min 19 mm.
- Emergency air: ≥ 19 cu ft cylinder, ≥ 15 min per crew member.
- Helmet: > 50 % hi-vis yellow / lime / orange / fluorescent red.
