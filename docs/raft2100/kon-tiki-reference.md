# Kon-Tiki Raft (1947) — Reference for Procedural 3D Build

Compiled 2026-08-21 by research agent. Primary source: Thor Heyerdahl, *The Kon-Tiki Expedition* (Allen & Unwin 1950), OCR at https://archive.org/details/dli.ernet.524366 — cited **[HEY p.N]**. Where secondary sources conflict with the book, the book wins.

Other sources: **[WP]** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kon-Tiki_expedition · **[WHE]** https://www.worldhistory.org/Kon-Tiki_Expedition/ · **[NOWP]** https://no.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C2%ABKon-Tiki%C2%BB · **[KTM-raft]** https://www.kon-tiki.no/no/news/flten-som-ingen-ville-ha · **[KTM-models]** https://www.kon-tiki.no/en/news/skalamodelleravkontiki · **[PHOTO-xx]** Wikimedia Commons photos of the actual raft (category `Kon-Tiki (ship, 1947)`), inspected visually; observations marked "(photo)".

## 1. Hull

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Main logs | **9** balsa logs (Ecuador, Quevedo; felled green) | [HEY p.65], [WP] |
| Centre log | **13.7 m** (45 ft), projecting at both ends | [HEY p.65] |
| Side logs | progressively shorter, symmetrical; **outermost 9.1 m** (30 ft); bow = V-stagger "blunt plough", stern cut square | [HEY p.65], [WHE] |
| Log diameter | up to **60 cm**; museum ends (dried) ~35 cm. Use 50–60 cm fresh, tapering | [WP], oslo-spirit guide |
| Bow ends | pointed/chamfered "native fashion"; **low pine splashboards** lashed above waterline at bow (~30–40 cm high, dark plank, shallow V) | [HEY p.65–66], [PHOTO-02,03] |
| Stern | logs square-cut; **three middle logs project ~0.6 m** to carry steering block | [HEY p.65] |
| Lashing | **30 mm hemp**, ~**300 separate lashings**, grooves cut into logs; no metal | [HEY p.65], [WP] |
| Gaps between logs | irregular, **~2–8 cm** (logs floated to natural position then lashed); water drains through | [HEY p.65, 83, 109] |
| Cross-beams | thin balsa logs athwartships on top of main logs, **~0.91 m** spacing, **9 beams**, ~**5.5 m long, ~30 cm Ø** | [HEY p.65, 70], [WP] |
| Foot-rails | one slim balsa log along each deck edge; no bulwark | [HEY p.66] |
| Deck | split bamboo strips + loose plaited bamboo mats | [HEY p.65] |
| Deck coverage | **forward of cabin + starboard strip alongside cabin** only. Port side of cabin = lashed boxes/gear with narrow walkway. Bow tips + everything aft of cabin = bare grey logs | [HEY p.66] |
| Under deck | ration cartons + **56 water cans** lashed between cross-beams under deck; **8 boxes** under cabin floor | [HEY p.70] |
| Overall | ~**13.7–14 m × 5–5.5 m** (beam inferred from cross-beam length) | |
| Freeboard | cabin sill ~**45 cm** above waterline, **1.5 m** in from unprotected edge; helmsman waist-deep in storms; rode lower as logs soaked | [HEY p.132, 147] |
| Draft | logs ~half submerged; guaras reach 1.5 m below | [HEY p.66] |
| Weight | ~20 t waterlogged (Oslo 1947) | [KTM-raft] |
| Deck detail (photo) | split canes laid athwartships, whole bamboo poles (~6–8 cm) fore-aft as battens ~1 m apart; woven mats (~3 cm strips) over fore-deck | [PHOTO-02,03,05,06,21] |

## 6. Deck items (1947)

- 56 water cans under deck (1,040 l); sealed bamboo water tubes. [HEY p.70]
- US Army ration cartons (khaki, printed) under deck + stacked; fresh supplies forward of cabin. [HEY p.70, 102]
- Wicker baskets of fruit/coconuts on foredeck; sweet-potato baskets in cabin lee. [HEY p.70, 103]
- Kitchen box w/ Primus, starboard outside door. [HEY p.102]
- Crates + gear along **port** cabin wall; 8 boxes under floor; nav box aft. [HEY p.66, 70, 81]
- **Yellow** ring-type rubber dinghy, on edge against port cabin wall. [HEY p.91], [PHOTO-04]
- Parrot cage under cabin roof. [HEY p.145]
- Paraffin hurricane lantern, hung on aft gable. [HEY p.82], [PHOTO-08]
- Radio: NC-173 receiver, 6 W transmitters, batteries, hand generator; aerial from mast. [WP]
- 3–4 hemp rope coils ~60 cm Ø on foredeck. [PHOTO-05,06]
- Harpoons, hand lines, plankton net (18 in ring) towed astern, diving basket in bows, lifebelt astern. [HEY p.106, 119, 152]
- Rope ladder up mast leg. Duty roster board. Pans/ladle on starboard cabin wall.

## 7. Colours / textures

- **Balsa**: fresh pale yellow-white → "round grey logs" at sea; museum grey-brown, deep end-grain checks, rope grooves, green weed along waterline.
- **Bamboo**: golden-yellow mats; cabin weave green+yellow new → greying tan.
- **Thatch**: leathery green banana leaf → dry brown; museum pale straw palm.
- **Rope**: 30 mm three-strand hemp, tan → grey-brown.
- **Sail**: off-white linen weathered to ochre; face rust-red, dark outline.
- **Guaras / splashboards / steering block**: dark weathered plank, rope through auger holes.
- **Boxes**: khaki ration cardboard, pine crates; yellow dinghy; Norwegian flag.

## 9. Unconfirmed / conflicting

1. Mast height 8.8 m vs "nearly 10 m"; leg spacing, pole Ø, lean angle — photo estimates only.
2. Sail 4.6×5.5 vs 4.5×5.5; hoist vs width unstated. Topsail/mizzen sizes unknown.
3. Sail colour: "orange" (book) vs tan/ochre (museum).
4. Guara count 5 (book) vs 6 (runasimi); positions never recorded.
5. Cross-beams "thin balsa" (book) vs pine; 9 beams likely under decked midsection only.
6. Oar 5.8 m (book) — 4.5 m "mango wood" figures are errors.
7. Beam 5–5.5 m inferred.
8. Door: starboard per book; museum rebuild 1948–50 may differ in size/position.
9. No dimensioned plan online.
10. Museum dressing (boxes, mattress, lantern, dinghy, tarp) is curatorial.
